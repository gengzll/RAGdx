Composite objective (RAG Bayesian search)

Metric weights combine into the composite score (sum of weight_i * metric_i) that the RAG-config Bayesian search optimizes and that the report uses for headline comparisons. The prompt optimizer (DSPy) selects its winner with its own inner-loop metric. Weights follow the practical priority hierarchy groundedness > correctness > retrieval quality > auxiliary judges. Lower-is-better metrics (hallucination / bias / toxicity) are inverted (1 - value) before weighting, so every weight rewards the healthy direction. Constraints are hard pass/fail thresholds: a trial that violates one is still scored but marked infeasible so the planner can filter unsafe configs.

Metric weights

faithfulness2.0Of the claims in the answer, how many are supported by the retrieved context. Low = the generator is making things up.
answer_relevancy1.5How directly the answer addresses the question (not off-topic or padded).
context_precision1.0Of the retrieved chunks, how many are actually relevant — and are the relevant ones ranked first. Low = the retriever is pulling noise.
g_eval0.5A chain-of-thought LLM judge scoring overall answer quality against a rubric (grounded + complete + on-topic).
hallucination0.5How much of the answer is fabricated / contradicts the context. Lower is better.
